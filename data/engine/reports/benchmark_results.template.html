Methods, uncertainty & sources

Same 20,000 tasks

5,000 per family, seed 69, v3 answer-only prompts, pinned Vidyut Prakriya 0.4.0 gold forms. OpenAI and Anthropic use xHigh; both Gemini models use HIGH. No tools, web access, conversation history, or benchmark output-token cap.

What ±1 SD means

Population standard deviation of the four task-family accuracy scores, in percentage points (N = 4; ddof = 0). Whiskers appear in the average view only, clipped to the 0–100% scale. They describe differences across families, not confidence intervals or variation across repeated runs.

Score and aggregation

Exact membership in accepted Devanagari answers, without Unicode or spelling normalization. Strict scoring also requires one nonempty <answer> block with only whitespace outside it. Average, maximum, and minimum combine family metrics independently; an average of family medians is not a pooled median.

Distributions and paired outcomes

Distribution plots use the selected family or all 20,000 responses pooled. Percentiles use linear interpolation. Paired outcomes compare matching task IDs; either-correct coverage describes the two observed answers, not an evaluated ensemble. Refusals and incomplete outputs count as failures.

Tokens, time, and spend

Output tokens include reported reasoning tokens. Duration is request elapsed time and depends on provider load and concurrency. Plotted cost uses selected attempts at pinned rates and the returned Google traffic tier, before credits. Earlier retries are excluded from plotted costs.

All attempts preserved

Recorded spend: , plus historical attempts with unknown charges. Every run has 20,000 finalized tasks. Argon has six incomplete outputs; Flash and Sol have one each; Sonnet has two refusals. No infrastructure failures remain. Astra v2 is excluded.
