METHOD
Seed 69. Freeze 40 unique previously cyber-refused benchmark tasks. Discovery: 10 tasks, each sent with the original prompt and three wording variants (40 requests). Select the changed variant with the fewest refusals, then completion/correctness tie-breaks. Holdout: 30 different tasks, each sent with the original and selected variant (60 requests). Randomized request order, concurrency 8. Same model, adaptive thinking, xhigh, 128000-token provider maximum; no tools or model fallback.
The 40 tasks comprise 32 krdanta, 4 tinanta, and 4 taddhitanta. Subanta was not tested because no refusals were available when the experiment was prepared. Original benchmark result files and production templates were not modified.

WHAT CHANGED
Replace the introductory role/instruction prose with the concise family-specific instructions in candidate-templates/. Keep every task field byte-for-byte, all required grammatical constraints, and the exact answer-only format instruction. No encoding, disguised fields, changed Sanskrit spellings, supplied gold answers, or safety-override instructions.
The combined introductory rewrite was tested; this experiment does not identify any single word as the cause of a classifier decision. Adding a linguistics preface or relabeling dictionary fields performed worse in discovery.

INTERPRETATION
The shorter instructions are a promising template candidate, especially for krdanta: 0/26 refusals on held-out krdanta versus 16/26 for the original. Tinanta still had 1/2 refusals for each wording; taddhitanta had 0/2 for each. The evidence for a benefit is therefore concentrated in krdanta, and the small nominal/verb-conjugation samples do not establish a general fix for those families.
Across discovery and holdout, the shorter version had 1/40 refusals and 38/40 correct answers; the original had 22/40 refusals and 17/40 correct answers. Holdout alone is the cleaner comparison because it was not used for selection.
These are deliberately selected previously refused tasks, so the observed rates do not estimate the full benchmark. Refusals were not fully eliminated. Unflagged tasks and subanta were not evaluated; a broader accuracy/refusal regression check would be needed before changing the shared production templates.
The old benchmark keeps its pinned prompts and original outcomes. Candidate templates are reviewable files only, not replacements for the active run.
