OUTCOME
The requested zero-refusal target was NOT achieved within the 100-request budget. Final confirmation had 1 cyber refusal in 20 requests, with 16 correct answers. The candidate templates have been updated in place with the tested final selections.

METHOD
Discovery: 40 calls comparing current wording with compact instructions, question-style instructions, and a dictionary-gloss preface. Four tinanta tasks and two tasks in each other category. All exact Sanskrit data fields and the answer-only instructions were retained.
The initial selection retained current wording on refusal/correctness ties. Paired stage: 20 fresh tasks, five per category, called twice (40 requests). The control and selected prompts were identical in this stage. These duplicate calls exposed stochastic refusals: identical prompts sometimes produced different outcomes.
After 80 calls, four paired-stage refusals on three tasks prompted an explicit protocol amendment. Tinanta switched to compact instructions; taddhitanta switched to question-style instructions. Krdanta and subanta retained their current wording. The original script, manifest and cases are preserved under protocol-history/.
Final confirmation: 20 calls, five per category, frozen before submission. It included 16 fresh tasks plus the three newly refused tasks and the earlier persistent tinanta task. The paired stage was used for tuning and therefore is not an untouched final validation set.
