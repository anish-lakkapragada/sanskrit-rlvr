## Revision delta and 7.3.32

Compared with revision01, 1,038 shared question IDs preserve identical grammar, gold and prompt; 3,762 IDs are new and 3,762 are removed. The 24 revision01 inputs excluded under current policy17 are absent. The global recipe, class and coordinate quotas, seed and family caps remain unchanged. Large membership movement follows the documented global allocation rerun and is not evidence of item-outcome selection.

The only new rule ID is **7.3.32**, in han 02.0002, `[yaNluk, Ric]`, VidhiLin/Kartari/Madhyama/Dvi. Kāśikā and Nyāsa prescribe final n→t before the relevant marked dhatu suffix, except ciṇ and ṇal, and directly give the ordinary causative ghātayati. Ric supplies that marked suffix here; the two exceptions and the nominal counterexample are absent. [Primary 7.3.32](https://sanskritdocuments.org/learning_tools/ashtadhyayi/vyakhya/7/7.3.32.htm).

The actual first change is `han → hAt`, followed by `hAt → GAt`. The engine groups vowel lengthening and n→t in one logged event; the immediately previous term is **not already lengthened**. The saved 5D control's original prose should be read with that minor correction. Penultimate a→ā is independently supported before ṇic, and the han velar rule's Nyāsa explicitly recognizes yaṅluk. The reduplication arose directly from han; no nominal hananīya detour appears. [7.2.116](https://sanskritdocuments.org/learning_tools/ashtadhyayi/vyakhya/7/7.2.116.htm), [7.3.54](https://sanskritdocuments.org/learning_tools/ashtadhyayi/vyakhya/7/7.3.54.htm), [7.3.55](https://sanskritdocuments.org/learning_tools/ashtadhyayi/vyakhya/7/7.3.55.htm).

Independent controls reproduce ordinary causal `GAtayati`, perfect `jaGAna`, and passive-aorist `aGAni`; the two exception controls do not apply 7.3.32. Complete candidate and control traces are saved. This is direct source evidence plus rule inference for the nested finite form, not a claim that its entire rare paradigm is independently printed.

The revision also introduces five recipe/rule combinations for yaṅluk→ṇic: 6.4.98, 7.3.32, 7.3.55, 8.4.55 and 8.4.64. The delta audit records them. This bounded review does not constitute a new exhaustive review of every previously known rule or rare accepted variant.
