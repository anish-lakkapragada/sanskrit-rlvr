Let Y indicate recorded correctness and C indicate usability. The four-state fit correctly preserves correct-but-nonusable rows separately. For allocation weights w, it computes q = E(C), a = E(Y), and θ = E(CY)/q. The usable influence is `Z = C*(Y−θ)/q`; nonusable observations therefore remain present with zero influence. The all-request influence uses Y. The reported iid variances are the marginal Bernoulli expressions `a*(1−a)/N` and `θ*(1−θ)/(N*q)`; these are references, not fixed-composition design-based variances.

For cell c, write its centered mean as δc and its conditional variance as vc. If ngc is the number of questions in cell c belonging to family g, the allocation's exact mean-square contribution is

`Σg (Σc ngc*δc)² = Σc nc*δc² + 2 Σunordered_pairs δa*δb`.

The code implements this identity using the full unordered same-family cell-pair counts, including same-cell pairs. The conditional variance contribution is `Σc nc*vc`. Under the stated category-correlation transport model, each unordered pair contributes `2*ρcategory*sqrt(va*vb)`. The coefficient construction and factors of two pass the independent direct-pair comparison. The semidefinite test correctly checks the two exchangeable Lun/non-Lun blocks through their block means and determinant; checking all sizes up to the caps is conservative relative to the actual allocation. Incoherent cases are flagged, even if their displayed aggregate variance is positive, and must not be treated as credible precision scenarios.

The field `expected_sandwich_se_pp` needs a precise reading: it is the square root of a **conditional plug-in second-moment forecast**, with the finite-family multiplier, centered at transported model means. It is not exactly E(SE), nor exactly the expectation of the finite-sample sandwich that re-centers at its random realized accuracy/ratio. For example, two equal families of two independent Bernoulli(½) observations give this forecast variance 0.125, whereas direct enumeration gives expected sample-centered sandwich variance 0.0625. This small-family example establishes the distinction; it does not quantify the discrepancy for the 949–971-family allocation witnesses. The family-mean identity above remains exact. A report should call the result a conditional/asymptotic precision forecast rather than an exact expected measured SE.
