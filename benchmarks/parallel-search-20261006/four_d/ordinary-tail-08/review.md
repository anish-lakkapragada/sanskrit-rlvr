The prospective complete-panel trigger requires **at least 90 usable responses, 16 wrong completed families, errors in three classes and four tenses**. Missing or truncated answers do not count as wrong completed families. Any apparent signal needs symmetric source review and later grammar-defined, independently confirmed expansion; individual failed items cannot become final membership. All 96 must settle before result access. At 79% the nominal iid standard error is about 4.2 percentage points. Wilson intervals and the family-ratio estimator are reported with their assumptions; the constrained panel is not a probability-weighted estimate of dictionary-wide accuracy.

The recommendation is one modest xHigh/65,536 Opus discovery screen, with no Astra pairing yet and no further generation request beyond these fixed 96. This can reduce a concrete uncertainty—whether broad, previously unmeasured ordinary families show distributed errors at the current reference protocol. Success would justify further research; failure would narrow that uncertainty. Neither outcome is an impossibility proof or benchmark completion.
