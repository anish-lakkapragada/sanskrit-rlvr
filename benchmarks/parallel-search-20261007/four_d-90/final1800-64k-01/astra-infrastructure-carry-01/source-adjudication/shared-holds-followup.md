## 229: an actual difference between commentary traditions

The decisive new evidence is *Kṣīrataraṅgiṇī*, **Bhvādigana entry667**, printed p.147. It gives **सरति** and then expressly applies3.1.56 to produce the aṅ aorist **असरत्**. The [scan204](https://jainqq.org/explore/002433/204) was visually inspected. This positively fixes the class: accepting the aṅ branch does not require silently changing Bhvādi to Juhotyādi.

Nyāsa on [7.4.16](https://sanskritdocuments.org/learning_tools/ashtadhyayi/vyakhya/7/7.4.16.htm) independently identifies **सृ गतौ**, entry935, when explaining **असरत्**. Kāśikā/Nyāsa on [3.1.56](https://sanskritdocuments.org/learning_tools/ashtadhyayi/vyakhya/3/3.1.56.htm) also gives the aṅ branch. The earlier uncertainty based on Kāśikā not naming a class is therefore resolved by stronger, class-specific evidence.

The requested plural follows a complete productive derivation:

1. Luṅ introduces cli;3.1.56 substitutes aṅ on this interpretation, and6.4.71 supplies initial aṭ.
2. Before aṅ,7.4.16 changes ṛ to ar: **a + sṛ + a → asara-**.
3. Third plural uses jhi;7.1.3 gives ant, and3.4.100 deletes the final i in this Ṅit lakāra. The adjacent a vowels coalesce by6.1.97; final nt loses t by8.2.23. The result is **असरन्**.

The existing **असार्षुः** remains supported. The visually inspected *Mādhavīyadhātuvṛtti*, printed pp.255–256 ([scan309](https://sanskritlibrary.org/Sanskrit/Vyakarana/Dhatupatha/mdhvscan/MDhVjpg/madhaviyahdhatuv00sayauoft_bw_0309.jpg), [scan310](https://sanskritlibrary.org/Sanskrit/Vyakarana/Dhatupatha/mdhvscan/MDhVjpg/madhaviyahdhatuv00sayauoft_bw_0310.jpg)), prints the corresponding sigmatic singular/dual and expressly restricts the aṅ prescription to Juhotyādi. The primary SK transcription agrees (`72011` and `31056`). On this branch, sic replaces cli, the aniṭ root receives vṛddhi under7.2.1, and third plural jhi becomes jus under3.4.109. Retroflexion and final visarga yield **a+sār+s+us → असार्षुः**.

The public prompt selects no commentary school. Both ordinary Bhvādi derivations should therefore be accepted. Both models returned **असरन्**. The panel contains exactly one task from this root family, so the current symmetric consequence is one additional accepted answer per model. This memo proposes the source correction; it does not apply a grade change. Other person/number cells require the same declared tradition policy and their own complete ending derivations, not unrestricted string substitutions.

## 437: lexical accent defect plus a missing nasal branch

The printed *Dhātupradīpa*, [scan44, p.34](https://jainqq.org/explore/004319/44), gives **जम्भते** under **जभ जृभि गात्रविनामे** and closes the section as middle verbs with anudātta it-markers. The complete form is already a direct witness, not an extrapolation from a causative.

*Daivam/Puruṣakāra*, [verse145 and its commentary](https://sa.wikisource.org/wiki/दैवम्_(पुरुषकारव्याख्यासमेतम्)), independently links ordinary yawning, **जम्भते**, the **जभ** reading, and7.1.61. Bālamanoramā on [7.1.61](https://sanskritdocuments.org/learning_tools/ashtadhyayi/vyakhya/7/7.1.61.htm) explicitly explains num before śap. These provide positive support for the submitted nasal form with the a-reading; its nasal need not come from an invented i-marker.

The derivation is: the source anudātta-it classification gives middle endings by1.3.12; ordinary Bhvādi present supplies vowel-initial śap;7.1.61 supplies num before that suffix, yielding **janbh**, then anusvāra and homorganic nasal rules8.3.24/8.4.58 give **jambh**. Third singular middle ta becomes te in Ṭit Laṭ by3.4.79. The result is **jambh+a+te → जम्भते**. No prefix, added sanādi operation or changed meaning is involved.

The historical prompt says **जभँ** (`jaBa~`), while the source-backed intrinsic middle citation would be **जभँ॒** (`jaBa~\`). The renderer preserves that distinction. Consequently this item needs a prospective input repair as well as a source-supported key; merely appending an answer leaves the defective input in place. No inspected source licenses the current P answer **जभति** for this exact ordinary yawning entry. Both models returned **जम्भते**, so there is no currently credited P response on this paired row, but the key must still be withdrawn on repair.

There is also a directly printed **जभते** witness: *Kṣīrataraṅgiṇī*, [scan122, p.65](https://jainqq.org/explore/002433/122), prints this nonnasal middle under the a-reading. Its [scan368, p.311](https://jainqq.org/explore/002433/368) refers back to the ordinary Bhvādi entry and prints the nasal form. Treat these as attributed textual branches. **Do not claim that Bālamanoramā makes its num prescription optional**: it requires the nasal in its derivation. The inspected Kṣīra page does not explain the rationale for its nonnasal finite stem.

The parent reports that VP generates only **जभते** after the accent-only repair. That engine result cannot invalidate directly printed **जम्भते**. A future key should include the independently reviewed source-supported middle branch or branches; a P+A union has no source basis. Preserve the historical row and mark the input defect rather than presenting old responses as an evaluation of a newly repaired prompt.

## 566: additional editions still do not establish ṣalṛ

Two further primary passages were visually inspected. *Mādhavīyadhātuvṛtti*, [scan204, p.150](https://sanskritlibrary.org/Sanskrit/Vyakarana/Dhatupatha/mdhvscan/MDhVjpg/madhaviyahdhatuv00sayauoft_bw_0204.jpg), distinguishes śel/sel with e from a separate ṣal entry; its discussion even distinguishes dental and retroflex initial readings. *Dhātupradīpa*, [scan52, p.42](https://jainqq.org/explore/004319/52), likewise separates the e-vowel group from sal. Neither establishes the supplied short-a **षलृँ** cited as a Kṣīra alternate.

This strengthens the existing input-identity hold without proving that no edition anywhere contains that reading. No complete licensed aorist derivation for **असलताम्** was found. Do not turn that absence into a certified error while the question's dictionary identity remains unverified.

## Practical limits
