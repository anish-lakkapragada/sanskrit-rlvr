## ऊन: the missing branch is real, but its complete release policy is not frozen

[Kāśikā and Nyāsa on 3.1.51](https://sanskritdocuments.org/learning_tools/ashtadhyayi/vyakhya/3/3.1.51.htm) distinguish the classical reduplicated **auni-** formation from the Vedic formation without caṅ. Nyāsa explicitly explains second-syllable reduplication and nic deletion for **औनिनत्**. This independently corroborates the photographed [Dhatupradipa entry and editorial note](https://jainqq.org/explore/004319/159). The Vedic forms printed nearby do not become valid answers to the classical task.

[Siddhāntakaumudī's ऊन entry](https://sa.wikisource.org/wiki/सिद्धान्तकौमुदी/प्रकरण‌५१-६०) expressly argues for the different timing that gives **औननत्**. The issue is therefore not resolved by saying that one visible form is a typo or by accepting only the current VP result. Both are supported classical commentary traditions.

## मिश्र: no justified aorist patch

The [Dhatupradipa scan](https://jainqq.org/explore/004319/162) reports the apparent **मिश्रति** reading immediately after **मिश्रयति**. The other inspected lexical commentaries give the aya formation. Independently, [Kāśikā on 3.1.21](https://sanskritdocuments.org/learning_tools/ashtadhyayi/vyakhya/3/3.1.21.htm) also gives **मिश्रयति** in the nominal “make mixed” construction.

That nominal explanation does not by itself license removing intrinsic nic from the exact Curādi task. This review found no primary derivation establishing how the reported simple present propagates to the proposed Luṅ questions. Nor does the failure to find another occurrence prove the reading invalid. Keep the uncertainty explicit and retain the family hold; neither an OCR-based correction nor an invented ordinary aorist is justified.

## सङ्ग्राम: the marker is part of lexical identity

[Bālamanoramā and Tattvabodhinī on 3.1.12](https://sanskritdocuments.org/learning_tools/ashtadhyayi/vyakhya/3/3.1.12.htm) explicitly discuss the separate additional a-marker, its relation to anudātta/ātmanepada behavior, and the need to retain the literal adanta vowel for the formation. This strengthens the earlier [Madhaviya evidence, printed page 573](https://sanskritlibrary.org/Sanskrit/Vyakarana/Dhatupatha/mdhvscan/disp/index.php?page=627).

The [Kshiratarangini entry 308](https://jainqq.org/booktext/Kshir_Tarangini/002433) nevertheless prints parasmaipada forms. Its editor expressly challenges them using Mahābhāṣya, Kaiyaṭa and Maitreya. Thus an ending-set restriction could be defensible within a declared source tradition, but the disagreement cannot be settled by filtering VP gold without documenting the dictionary and marker policy. The current visible citation `sangrAma` does not explicitly encode that additional marker. Excluding this lexical family avoids silently adding a choice to the four-coordinate task.
