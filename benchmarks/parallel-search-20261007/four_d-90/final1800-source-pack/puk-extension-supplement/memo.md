## Lexical scope: what “all kathādi” covers

The [Siddhāntakaumudī Curādi section](https://sa.wikisource.org/wiki/सिद्धान्तकौमुदी/प्रकरण‌५१-६०) begins its adanta discussion with कथ and continues through the later listed adanta examples. The existing inspected printed p.238 and [Bālamanoramā p.308](https://sa.wikisource.org/wiki/पृष्ठम्:सिद्धान्तकौमुदी_(बालमनोरमा_उत्तर-२).djvu/९४) expressly say: “शाकटायनस्तु कथादीनां सर्वेषां पुकमाह”. There is no person, number, or tense restriction in that sentence. The Bālamanoramā identifies Śākaṭāyana as an ṛṣi; this is an attributed alternative derivation inside the same ordinary Curādi discussion, not evidence of an extra causative coordinate.

All **35 selected hard families / 1,080 questions** occur in that adanta discussion. Twenty-six occur at or before the sentence: kaTa, vara, gaRa, paWa, raha, gada, svara, caha, spfha, BAma, goma, kumAra, sAma, vela, kAla, vAta, grAma, pada, kuha, garva, sUtra, tIra, katra, citra, ansa, laja. Nine follow the sentence within the continuing discussion: Cidra, anDa, danqa, anka, anga, duHKa, Cada, vraRa, vizka. The later text expressly describes its examples as adanta-dhātu illustrations and supplies further examples; it does not introduce a new root class at laja.

It is consequently reasonable to investigate the puK branch throughout those35 families. **Membership in the general kathādi reference is an inference for roots other than the named examples**, especially the nine following the sentence; this memo does not pretend that each individual puK form is printed. Nor does “all kathādi” mean every Curādi root, every a-sounding citation, every extra causative, or every nominal derivative. The JSON records each exact dictionary entry, position, selected tenses and existing intrinsic branches.

Two restrictions matter immediately. The ordinary pad/kuh entries lie in the explicit middle-only ā-garva range: a puK derivation cannot discard that restriction. Garva/katra retain their existing intrinsic ṇic/no-ṇic distinctions; puK before ṇic does not apply to a branch without ṇic. Also, the nearby printed लञ्जापयति is explained through the alternate **laji** reading. It does not by itself license inserting a nasal into the task's literal **laja** citation. Cada likewise retains the previously declared literal-final-a reading.
