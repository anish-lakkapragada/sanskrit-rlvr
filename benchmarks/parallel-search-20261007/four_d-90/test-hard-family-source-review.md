## Other distinctions that matter

**10.0481 छद is not a demonstrated invalid short stem.** Although the Siddhantakaumudi transcription gives a conflicting long-vowel form in its corresponding alternative entry, Kshiratarangini entry 321 explicitly gives **छदयति** and distinguishes other छद readings. The literal-adanta task therefore has direct primary support. Its edition/reading must be documented; that does not certify the 47 proposed forms or settle every cross-edition alternative. [Primary lexical commentary](https://jainqq.org/booktext/Kshir_Tarangini/002433), [Madhaviya variant entry, printed page 575](https://sanskritlibrary.org/Sanskrit/Vyakarana/Dhatupatha/mdhvscan/disp/index.php?page=629).

For **लज**, primary sources distinguish the literal-a reading from nasal-marker alternatives. **काल** is expressly permitted as a separate lexeme beside वेल. **छिद्र** has competing meaning/lexeme divisions, with the supplied meaning selecting one documented reading. **अंस** and palatal **अंश** should not be merged as free spelling variants. **चित्र**'s separately derived nominal construction should not be inserted into the gold merely because its surface meaning is related. These are lexical/analysis distinctions, not new Cartesian coordinates. [Siddhantakaumudi's adanta section](https://sa.wikisource.org/wiki/सिद्धान्तकौमुदी/प्रकरण‌५१-६०), [Madhaviya printed page 573](https://sanskritlibrary.org/Sanskrit/Vyakarana/Dhatupatha/mdhvscan/disp/index.php?page=627).

The **गर्व** and **कत्र** representative checks preserve the explicitly discussed intrinsic nic/no-nic alternatives. **पद** and **कुह** retain their intrinsic Atmanepada restriction. These restrictions belong to the lexical entry; the prompt does not secretly choose a fifth coordinate. [Madhaviya printed page 571](https://sanskritlibrary.org/Sanskrit/Vyakarana/Dhatupatha/mdhvscan/disp/index.php?page=625), [Siddhantakaumudi](https://sa.wikisource.org/wiki/सिद्धान्तकौमुदी/प्रकरण‌५१-६०).

## Scope of the checks

All 39 entries have empty explicit prefix and added-sanadi lists, pass the existing visible-identity ambiguity check, and survive existing shared/local source exclusions. Their class-intrinsic nic behavior is part of ordinary Curadi conjugation. A lexical compound such as सङ्ग्राम is supplied as the dictionary root, rather than silently assembled using an independently selected prefix.

The audit checked representative third-person singular present/aorist forms against the sources. **58 directly supported representative checks passed**, with one additional short-stem check for पठ resting on its explicit lexical entry plus rule inference. Not every family has a directly printed aorist in the inspected sources. Sources supporting the lexical stem and [6.4.48](https://sanskritdocuments.org/learning_tools/ashtadhyayi/vyakhya/6/6.4.48.htm), [7.4.2](https://sanskritdocuments.org/learning_tools/ashtadhyayi/vyakhya/7/7.4.2.htm), and [7.4.93](https://sanskritdocuments.org/learning_tools/ashtadhyayi/vyakhya/7/7.4.93.htm) motivate other forms by grammatical inference. They do not independently certify all 46–47 non-Lun questions per short family or every accepted spelling/pada variant.

No unsupported finite-use restriction was identified for the remaining roots. This means none was found in this bounded review, not that every alternative commentary has been exhausted. Cross-school variants should not be silently combined into a supposedly uniform Paninian gold set. The strongest concrete new omission is the classical ऊन aorist alternative; the mixed-source सङ्ग्राम and मिश्र findings require adjudication rather than an automatic scorer patch.

Recommended next step: preserve the proposal, resolve the three held families and मार्ग classification, then revise membership or oracle policy prospectively and regenerate before any paid held-out evaluation. Apply any exclusion to all affected questions independent of outcomes. The completed development panel and its 175/200 score are untouched by this review.
