## Fairness of the paired comparison

The strongest feature is exact target matching: each pair has the same underlying target coordinates and full gold union. Both arms use Sanskrit instructions, one-word Devanagari answer tags, xHigh effort and a65,536-token output ceiling. Compare modification against its paired `cartesian-4d-v3-sanskrit` controls, **not directly against the separately concentrated English-prompt 4D-90 pilot**. The latter differs in root distribution, tense mix and elicitation language.

The intervention intentionally replaces dhatu/class/meaning with a conjugated source word and source coordinates. It does not isolate only “removing the dhatu,” and a performance gap cannot by itself identify whether lexical inference, reading a rare source form, or target generation caused the difference. That is acceptable for this operational benchmark, provided the interpretation remains modest.

Each arm covers200distinct development families. Target tenses are balanced, with10%Lun; source tenses are restricted to Lat/Lit/Lan/Lun,25%each.
